1. Static charts just show the data as a picture. 
   Interactive charts, like with Plotly and Dash, let you click, hover, and choose options to explore the data.
2. Dashboards let you explore data in real time. You can filter and interact with charts to see patterns and changes quickly,
   without making new graphs each time.
3. Plotly and Dash help people understand data better. You can click, filter, and explore charts,
   so it's easier to see patterns and explain results to other.
